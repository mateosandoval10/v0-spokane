Activation Strategy

Each concept is framed as a practical sponsorship platform: why the partner fits, how the fan experience works, and what USL Spokane can measure.

Evaluation Criteria

What made an activation worth recommending?

Audience fit

Does the sponsor naturally reach families, supporters, youth clubs, community groups, or active Spokane consumers?

Matchday behavior

Can the idea create a clear action fans understand during the match, not just a logo placement?

Local relevance

Does the concept connect to Spokane, the Pacific Northwest, or a visible community need?

Measurability

Can USL Spokane track redemptions, scans, attendance lift, group sales, content engagement, or community impact?
